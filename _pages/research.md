My research explores biodiversity through ecological and evolutionary perspectives, with particular interests in wildlife parasitology, evolutionary genomics, and conservation biology.

Natural history collections are central to my work, providing an invaluable resource for investigating biodiversity across space and time.

## Current Research Projects

### Northwest Territories Gray Wolf Endoparasites

Investigating gastrointestinal endoparasite diversity in gray wolves (*Canis lupus*) from the Northwest Territories, Canada, and its relationship with host ecology and wildlife management.

[Learn more →]({{ '/research/nwt-wolves/' | relative_url }})

### Mexican Gray Wolf Endoparasites

Investigating gastrointestinal helminth diversity and parasite surveillance in endangered Mexican gray wolves (*Canis lupus baileyi*) from the southwestern United States.

[Learn more →]({{ '/research/mexican-wolves/' | relative_url }})

### North American Least Weasel Coat Color

Exploring seasonal coat color variation in North American least weasels (*Mustela nivalis*) using  genomic data from museum study skins.

[Learn more →]({{ '/research/least-weasels/' | relative_url }})

### Texas Carnivore Fecal Metagenomics

Using fecal metagenomics to investigate dietary diversity and endoparasite communities across carnivores from West Texas.

[Learn more →]({{ '/research/texas-carnivores/' | relative_url }})

### Eurasian Shrew Phylogeography

Understanding how Eurasian shrews (Genus *Sorex*) diversified amid climate oscillations in Europe and Asia.

[Learn more →]({{ '/research/shrews/' | relative_url }})
